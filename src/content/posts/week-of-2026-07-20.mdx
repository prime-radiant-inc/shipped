Two releases anchor the week: **superpowers** cut `v6.2.0`, rolling up weeks of methodology work, and **winpepper** shipped a pair of alpha releases that quietly turned a local dictation tool into one that can also reach a cloud recognizer. Underneath the releases, **serf** logged its single heaviest push of the whole window — and several of the week's merged fixes came from outside the core team.

## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers)

<p class="repo-desc">An agentic skills framework and software-development methodology that works.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="superpowers" week="2026-07-20" />

`v6.2.0` is the week's headline release, gathering up work that had been merging over the prior weeks: the spec-driven-development plan-scoped workspace with its resume-based fix loop, a skills-compression sweep, and a Windows `SessionStart` fix that dispatches through Git-Bash. Two outside contributors landed fixes folded into the same release — a `find-polluter.sh` path-matching correction and a dangling-docs-anchor cleanup — a small sign of a framework picking up hands beyond the core team.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [obra/winpepper](https://github.com/obra/winpepper)

<p class="repo-desc">Windows-native local dictation: hold a hotkey, speak, release, and cleaned-up words appear in the focused app — Parakeet TDT v3 ASR with LlamaSharp cleanup, all local.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="winpepper" week="2026-07-20" />

The week read like a reliability pass — load-bearing-validation hardening for the mid-paste and settings-change races — but it culminated in two tagged alpha releases, and the release-grounded story is bigger than the diligence underneath it. AssemblyAI cloud ASR now sits alongside the local engine, sleep/resume incidents got a proper fix (an error taxonomy, recovery-driven clearing, hook reinstallation), the keyboard hook was hardened, and the app gained a per-user MSI installer plus a round of consumer polish (toast policy, pill fixes, ASR-config consolidation, live model swapping). As with superpowers, most of the commits came from outside — Dan Shapiro, with AI coding agents (Codex, Amplifier) committing alongside him.

</article>

### [prime-radiant-inc/serf](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/serf)

<p class="repo-desc">A non-interactive coding agent: give it a prompt and it reads, writes, runs commands, and searches code in a loop until the work is done, using native tool-calling across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google models.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="serf" week="2026-07-20" />

serf's busiest week of the window, almost all of it direct-push work on the session and pane architecture — a delegate-transcript UI audit, pane-routing repairs, nested-session owner promotion, and settings/session-routing placement contracts. Four concrete features merged on top of that rebuild: forking any message into the composer for editing, folding archived sessions behind a single disclosure grouped by project, prepopulating the session-path dropdown with recent projects, and per-turn job-notification parsing. Also in the mix: model-switch replay-parity work (asserting the same event kind in both live and replay paths) and serffuzz compile-gate fixes.
