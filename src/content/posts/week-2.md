Two projects were born this week and superpowers cut two releases — most of the energy went into making Codex a first-class harness.

<section class="featured">

## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) — v6.1.0, v6.1.1

<p class="repo-desc">An agentic skills framework and software-development methodology that actually works.</p>

`v6.1.0` (Jun 30) and `v6.1.1` (Jul 2) were largely about Codex: packaging a Codex portal, preserving hooks in the Codex manifest, and fixing the `SessionStart` hook re-registration that had been double-firing. If you're running superpowers under Codex rather than Claude Code, this is the week it started behaving.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/agent-plugin-linear-use](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/agent-plugin-linear-use)

<p class="repo-desc">A Claude Code / Codex skill + hook that finds or creates a Linear ticket and moves it through your workflow — a public example of how Prime Radiant works.</p>

New this week. It's also a deliberate artifact: a working, public example of keeping AI-driven work tracked in Linear the way we do internally.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [obra/insanitty](https://github.com/obra/insanitty)

<p class="repo-desc">A native Linux (GTK4/libadwaita) terminal workspace manager — a port of Fantastty built on embedded Ghostty, with tmux-backed workspaces and a QUIC remote engine.</p>

New this week and already moving fast: 90 commits out of the gate.

</article>
