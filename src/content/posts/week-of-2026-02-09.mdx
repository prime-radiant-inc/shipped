## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/serf](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/serf)

<p class="repo-desc">An agent framework — multi-provider, session-persistent, with a plugin system and an evaluation harness.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="serf" week="2026-02-09" />

The defining project of the week. serf began by importing an earlier codebase (Kilroy) as its foundation, rebranding it, and then building outward fast: a CLI with non-interactive task execution, a canonical error hierarchy, an OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions adapter alongside the Anthropic path, streaming structured output, session snapshots with `--resume`/`--list-sessions`, a bundled LiteLLM model catalog, a plugin system with plugin-defined agent types and hook lifecycle events, and a growing SWE-bench evaluation harness (Codex and GPT-5.2 like-for-like runs with failure-to-pass scoring). A 344-commit week from Jesse Vincent — one of the largest single-week pushes in Prime Radiant's history.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [obra/wayback-restorer](https://github.com/obra/wayback-restorer)

<p class="repo-desc">A general Wayback Machine recovery CLI — discover, capture, and rebuild a browsable mirror.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="wayback-restorer" week="2026-02-09" />

Grew into a general recovery tool across nineteen commits: CDX discovery with resume-key pagination, canonical capture selection, internal-link rewriting for standalone mirror browsing, provenance logging, and atomic writes. Sole contributor: Jesse Vincent.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/beeper-message-sync](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/beeper-message-sync)

<p class="repo-desc">A Swift tool that archives Beeper chats to per-chat JSONL with attachments and contact resolution.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="beeper-message-sync" week="2026-02-09" />

Landed across thirty-one commits: per-chat JSONL logging with attachments, contact resolution, and both backfill and watch modes. Sole contributor: Jesse Vincent.

</article>
