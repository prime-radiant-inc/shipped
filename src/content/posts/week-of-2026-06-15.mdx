A week of releases landing. **Superpowers 6** shipped its full release train, **claude-session-driver** cut `v4.0.0` — the release that ships the bash-to-TypeScript rewrite from the week before — and **obol** and **slackline** both stepped their versions. This is the last of the weeks we're reaching back to fill in; the [week of June 22](/shipped/posts/week-of-2026-06-22/) picks up right after it as our first originally-published recap.

## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers)

<p class="repo-desc">An agentic skills framework and software-development methodology that actually works.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="superpowers" week="2026-06-15" />

A full Superpowers 6 release train in one week: `v6.0.0` unified the evals submodule across per-agent bootstrap scenarios and brought Prime Radiant branding to the visual companion; `v6.0.1` fixed Codex plugin-version display; `v6.0.2` stopped shipping the evals submodule after it turned out to break plugin installs; and `v6.0.3` moved spec-driven-development artifacts out of the git-protected `.git/` path into the working tree, with per-worktree isolation tests. (A "Job posting" commit also landed mid-week — recruiting, not code.)

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [obra/claude-session-driver](https://github.com/obra/claude-session-driver)

<p class="repo-desc">Launch, control, and monitor other Claude Code sessions as workers via tmux.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="claude-session-driver" week="2026-06-15" />

`v4.0.0` — the release that ships the previous week's full TypeScript core rewrite (Claude parity plus Codex and Pi drivers). It came with follow-on fixes: Codex `apply_patch` rendering in `read-turn`, Pi tool-name casing normalized to the Bash/Read convention, an unregistered-derive-workers listing, a `--last N` cap on `read-events --follow` backlog, and a mixed-fleet `HARNESS` column in `list`.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/obol](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/obol)

<p class="repo-desc">Reads an AI-agent transcript and estimates what it cost — a Rust core with bindings for Python, Go, and TypeScript.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="obol" week="2026-06-15" />

Releases `v0.5.0` and `v0.6.0`. obol added an ATIF trajectory dialect and then — a breaking change — removed the per-agent raw-log dialects entirely, leaving ATIF and obol's own dialect as the only two supported formats. Plus a cost-accuracy fix: an empty model now signals "unknown" rather than "unpriced," and an explicit `final_metrics.total_cost_usd` overrides the rate-table lookup when present.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/slackline](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/slackline)

<p class="repo-desc">A single-binary Go CLI that gives AI agents a Slack identity.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="slackline" week="2026-06-15" />

Releases `v0.4.0` and `v0.5.0`, mostly user-facing polish: `@handle` mention linkification (in both `read` and `ask`), user-ID resolution, a new `users` command, an `auth whoami`, and message permalinks. It also defaulted `read`/`listen` output to compact text and fixed a dropped `message_replied` parent-event bug plus a Socket Mode listener-failure visibility gap.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/clipfan](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/clipfan)

<p class="repo-desc">A clipboard-sync daemon for a Mac plus a remote tmux fleet.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="clipfan" week="2026-06-15" />

A small, focused `v1.0.9` after the big 1.0 push: an idle-CPU regression fixed, and tmux fanout guarded against stale buffer hooks.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [obra/private-journal-mcp](https://github.com/obra/private-journal-mcp)

<p class="repo-desc">An MCP server for a private journaling capability.</p>

<RepoStat org="obra" name="private-journal-mcp" week="2026-06-15" />

Two releases went out this week, `v2.0.0` and `v2.0.1` — but honestly, that's the whole story here: the underlying code landed in an earlier week, and these were the tags being cut. We'd rather say that plainly than invent a feature narrative the week's data doesn't support.

</article>

A "session-state architecture" pass: an O(tail) incremental turn-projection cache that avoids full-log rescans, a cross-process monotonic sequence authority (flock plus a head file, with self-healing detection of pid reuse on stale locks), and a non-lossy verbatim `event_journal` recall store.
