The most recent week in this batch, and it leans toward tooling that helps *other* tools ship. **everyharness** (generate a coding-agent plugin for every harness from one config) and its companion **everyharness-container** went public, as did **proving-it-works** (make a movie that proves your software actually runs). Around them, superpowers cut `v6.3.0`, clipfan and agentic-usage-meter both released, and serf ran a sweeping UX pass — several changes landing from outside contributors, including a couple of recognizable names. This week is still open: the data below runs through mid-week, not the full calendar week.

## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/everyharness](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/everyharness)

<p class="repo-desc">Generate a coding-agent plugin for every harness from one config file.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="everyharness" week="2026-08-10" />

Creation to a breaking "config v2" in the same week — tagged bootstrap, per-harness settings, a release section — plus per-harness `emitHooks` control, exec-bit preservation checks for installed skill scripts, a `bump` command that replaces per-repo version-bump scripts, and byte-exact dogfood regeneration against hand-written manifests. The tell that it's real: two other repos (`the-elements-of-style`, `proving-it-works`) adopted it the same week it was born. Load-bearing on day one.

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/proving-it-works](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/proving-it-works)

<p class="repo-desc">Make a movie that proves your software actually works — three recording routes plus a checker that catches frozen pictures, desynced narration, and dropped words before you ship.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="proving-it-works" week="2026-08-10" />

Built end-to-end in days: three recording routes, an assembler, subtitles by default with a local voice fallback when there's no API key, and a checker that flags frozen frames, desynced narration, and dropped words. It shipped a demo of itself, and picked up `everyharness` support the same week — a tool for proving software works, proving itself works.

</article>

### [prime-radiant-inc/everyharness-container](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/everyharness-container)

<p class="repo-desc">A multi-harness container with ~17 coding-agent CLIs preinstalled, shared by everyharness and superpowers-evals.</p>

<RepoStat org="prime-radiant-inc" name="everyharness-container" week="2026-08-10" />

Extracted from `superpowers-evals` into its own shared image: the exact CLI inventory documented, an image-size and platform note, and a first-build digest. Infrastructure de-duplication — one image two projects can lean on instead of two drifting copies.
