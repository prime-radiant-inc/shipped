Two new repos this week, and they're a study in contrast: one is governance, the other is a menu-bar utility that was clearly built in a hurry.

<section class="featured">

## Featured

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/code-of-conduct](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/code-of-conduct)

<p class="repo-desc">The Prime Radiant Community Code of Conduct.</p>

New this week. As more of our work goes public, having a stated code of conduct in its own repo — adaptable and linkable — is part of doing that properly.

</article>

<article class="repo">

### [prime-radiant-inc/agentic-usage-meter](https://github.com/prime-radiant-inc/agentic-usage-meter)

<p class="repo-desc">A macOS menu-bar meter for coding-agent subscription quotas.</p>

Created Aug 2 and immediately busy — 136 commits in its first days, with a run of releases starting the same week. If you're juggling quota across multiple coding-agent subscriptions, this is the little gauge that tells you where you stand.

</article>
