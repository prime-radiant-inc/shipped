Simon Willison's external-sandbox family tags three hardening releases locking the managed proxy down by default, and evener posts a release-free week of quota-wait, reasoning-replay, and test-suite cleanup

Week of November 16, 2026 · Nov 13, 23:55 – Nov 20, 23:05 UTC · published Nov 20, 2026

prime-radiant-inc/evener

A coding agent: give it a prompt and it reads, writes, runs commands, and searches code in a loop until the work is done, using native tool-calling across OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google models.

380 commits 382 PRs +210,537 −33,550

By far the busiest repo this window, as in every prior week, but with no tagged release. A reliability cluster around “quota wait” bookkeeping continues: reconciling waits by timestamp across a read cut, keeping quota-wait stamps monotonic across restores, dropping a legacy unstamped fallback, and rendering the pending half of a quota wait on the pane and row. Session/transcript handling gets a parallel push: replaying tool-call argument bytes losslessly across restore, settling Responses-API calls and reasoning parts consistently, pricing only the reasoning the Responses wire actually sends, and converging the live reasoning preview with reassembled details by item identity — aimed at keeping cross-provider LLM fallback coherent. A security fix confines the sandbox’s restricted Seatbelt outbound network access to explicitly granted roots. The week’s largest single thread is test-suite cleanup: a long run of commits drops duplicate and tautological tests across the agent and hub layers, removes dead skips and compile-only test wrappers, and replaces fixed timeouts with budget-aware waits. In-org author: Jesse Vincent (staff, obra), plus outside contributions from Asish (NopeCharon) and a first-time outside contributor, Huyvux12.

prime-radiant-inc/agent-session-archiver
