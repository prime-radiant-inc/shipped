Scribble comes online, faster VM boots, llm-proxy v0.2.0

Week of January 19, 2026 · Jan 19, 2026–Jan 25, 2026 · published Jan 25, 2026

This was the week Prime Radiant’s Slack presence started to take shape — the assistant bot landed its first real orchestrator, the microVM stack roughly halved its boot time, and llm-proxy cut a Loki-export release.

Featured

prime-radiant-inc/scribble

A Slack-native assistant bot — the coworker that lives in the workspace.

68 commits +20,926 −2,490

Scribble landed its first real orchestrator across a sixty-eight-commit week: a text-file StateStore (SQLite retired for EFS compatibility), an AttentionTracker for engagement, and a three-stage message pipeline (classify → extract → assemble). A wiki-editing toolset arrived with a “WikiGardener” that proposes maintenance and waits for confirmation, Linear ticketing came in over the StreamLinear MCP, structured JSON logging and OpenTelemetry metrics went in, and the bot moved onto Claude Sonnet 4.5. Jesse Vincent drove nearly all of it, with one commit from Drew Ritter.

prime-radiant-inc/stockyard

A microVM-orchestration daemon — ZFS-backed, Flintlock-driven Firecracker VM management over a gRPC API.

17 commits +4,012 −95

With the foundation in place, the week went to boot performance — cutting VM boot from roughly 6.2s to 3.1s by starting stockyard-init earlier and auditing systemd as the bottleneck — plus Tailscale node pre-registration so VMs come up already on the network. Sole contributor: Jesse Vincent.

prime-radiant-inc/llm-proxy

A Go reverse proxy that sits in front of LLM APIs and logs the traffic.

13 commits 🏷 v0.2.0 +5,503 −94

Shipped v0.2.0, adding Loki log export with async batching and a MultiWriter fan-out, a /health/loki endpoint, and config auto-discovery — thirteen commits from Drew Ritter, who also updated the Homebrew tap to match.
