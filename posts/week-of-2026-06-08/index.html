clipfan reaches 1.0, and a research-methodology stack takes shape

Week of June 8, 2026 · Jun 8, 2026–Jun 14, 2026 · published Jun 14, 2026

clipfan crossed v1.0.0 this week, finishing its self-healing mesh. Three new repos appeared: a live claude-plugin-stats scraper, and a pair — superpowers-autoresearch and superpowers-docs — that together put a RED/GREEN evidence-battery methodology to work on real projects. Behind them, the eval lab did the heavy lifting of the week: a full port of its Quorum harness to TypeScript.

The week in numbers

  • 29 repos active (6 featured, 23 also shipped)
  • 1,553 commits
  • 52 merged PRs
  • +428,650 / −101,182 lines
  • 7 contributors
  • 20 tagged releases

As in prior weeks, read the two headline counts with a grain of salt. Part of the active-repo count is a one-off CI-maintenance sweep (a GitHub Actions Node-20-runner deprecation bump) that touched many otherwise-quiet repos, and the tagged-release count is dominated by clipfan and obol, both of which tag a release per merged change. The substantive work is in the repos below.

prime-radiant-inc/superpowers-autoresearch

An internal research-methodology tool for the superpowers project.

17 commits +85,958 −9

New this week — a rigorous research format: a RED/GREEN evidence battery with pre-registration, a held-out battery, and blinded re-grading. Its first live run, on a “writing-skills” experiment, turned up a counter-intuitive backfire result — the kind of finding the method exists to catch, where the intervention made things worse and the discipline of the protocol is what surfaced it honestly.
