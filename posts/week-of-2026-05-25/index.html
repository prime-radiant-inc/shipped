clipfan launches, winpepper finds its voice

Week of May 25, 2026 · May 25, 2026–May 31, 2026 · published May 31, 2026

Two launches anchor the week. clipfan appeared and shipped its first tagged release in the same seven days — a clipboard-sync daemon that mirrors your Mac’s pasteboard to a remote fleet — and winpepper, the local Windows dictation tool, cut a v0.6.1-alpha in its first full week of life. Around them, a wide spread of repos stayed busy across prime-radiant-inc and obra: eval-lab run economics, a VM-orchestrator backend swap, and a fail-closed token substituter in the logging proxy.

Featured

prime-radiant-inc/clipfan

A clipboard-sync daemon for a Mac plus a remote tmux fleet — mirrors the macOS pasteboard to remote OS clipboards and tmux paste buffers, so you can paste images into Claude Code or Codex over SSH without OSC 52 or Xvfb.

131 commits 🏷 v0.3.0 +24,043 −2,492

New this week, and it didn’t waste any time: created and shipped its first tagged release, v0.3.0, in the same window. The core is a daemon plus menubar app with clip-ID-based echo suppression — mint an ID at the origin, suppress echoes of your own writes, dedup by ID — so sync doesn’t loop. On top of that came a menubar overhaul (a Settings window with Fleet/General/Diagnostics tabs, per-Mac fleet rows, daemon version reporting), configurable history limits and a global-shortcut recorder, and a full CI signing/notarization pipeline with Sparkle auto-update — all in one week. A security-hardening pass on the sync and install paths landed ahead of the release.

obra/winpepper

Windows-native local dictation — hold a hotkey, speak, release. Parakeet TDT v3 ASR plus LlamaSharp cleanup, entirely local.

15 commits 9 PRs 🏷 v0.6.1-alpha +471 −107

Its first full week after being created, and it shipped a v0.6.1-alpha. The week’s work: a “Lab” test harness (media transport controls, custom prompt cleanup, a minimal play/mute/seek control), an About-screen activation fix, local-time diagnostic timestamps, and CI hardening (three pre-existing failures fixed, doc-only PRs skipped, a debounce-coalesce test flake resolved). Most of it came from community contributor Nat Torkington, who opened and authored the bulk of the merged PRs; Jesse reviewed and merged.
