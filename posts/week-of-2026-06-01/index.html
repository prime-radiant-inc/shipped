obol arrives, clipfan grows a self-healing mesh

Week of June 1, 2026 · Jun 1, 2026–Jun 7, 2026 · published Jun 7, 2026

A launch-heavy week. obol — a cost estimator that reads an agent transcript and tells you what it cost, with bindings for Python, Go, and TypeScript — appeared and shipped a v0.1.1 with a full multi-channel release pipeline behind it. homedir-manager split out of the dotfiles repo into a standalone deploy-and-audit engine. And a run of releases landed across slackline, clearance, and clipfan, the last of which grew a self-healing SSH mesh.

The week in numbers

  • 44 repos active (6 featured, 38 also shipped)
  • 962 commits
  • 15 merged PRs
  • +266,196 / −80,333 lines
  • 11 contributors
  • 36 tagged releases

Two numbers to read carefully this week. The active-repo count is inflated by an org-wide housekeeping commit — a single “project-map” doc added to dozens of otherwise-quiet repos — so many of those repos saw no feature work. And the tagged-release count is dominated by clipfan, which tags a release per merged change (29 patch tags this week alone); that’s a packaging cadence, not 29 distinct milestones. The real feature work is in the repos called out below.

obra/homedir-manager

A symlink-deploy and audit tool for dotfiles content repos, plus a macOS defaults helper (POSIX sh and Swift).

36 commits +5,845 −147

New this week — the “management machinery” carved out of obra/dotfiles into its own standalone engine: multi-repo discovery, deploy and audit engines, and a marker-based content-repo registry. prime-radiant-inc/clipfan

A clipboard-sync daemon for a Mac plus a remote tmux fleet.

169 commits 🏷 29 releases +64,083 −2,811
