The work clusters into a few sustained threads rather than one single feature: a large “copy-oracle” test-hardening campaign spanning nearly the whole week and touching web, native, hub, agent, TUI, sandbox, frontend, and appwire-client tests — converting assertions on literal copy/wording into assertions on structural state, markers, error sentinels/types, or toast kinds, backed by new tooling to catch regressions of the same kind (a copy-oracle inventory of wording-only tests, a CI ratchet against new ones, and that inventory tool itself later ported from Python to Go and TypeScript); a web-UI migration onto the shared widget library, done in explicit waves with a final follow-up and “last leftovers” pass; device/mobile pairing work (shared device invitations plus native Hub pairing, pairing destinations kept visible on narrow screens, automatic Hub-destination discovery for mobile pairing, clarified phone QR/sound-pairing codes); a per-test timing “gate” hardened incrementally to fail loudly on a malformed testing-budget config instead of silently, then segmented by test class, with some runner-bound classes returned to warn-only per a dated ruling; and entry-backed background transcripts, landed as a feature and then followed by a repair pass for defects the review found.
