Building for a community: a code of conduct and a usage meter

Week of July 27, 2026 · Jul 27, 2026–Aug 2, 2026 · published Aug 2, 2026

No releases this week — but two brand-new repositories that both point outward. A community code of conduct signals that these projects expect outside contributors, and agentic-usage-meter, a macOS menu-bar app for tracking coding-agent subscription quotas, went public. Underneath the new repos, serf logged another heavy direct-push week and a research campaign in superpowers-autoresearch closed out with a measured, substantial cost cut.

prime-radiant-inc/code-of-conduct

5 commits +161 −30

A small repo with an outsized signal: the Contributor Covenant (v3) adapted into a Prime Radiant community code of conduct, refined after review feedback.
