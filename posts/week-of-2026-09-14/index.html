teststrip ships v0.3.0 and v0.3.1, agentic-usage-meter cuts v0.2.6, and clearance pulls in four outside contributors in one week

Week of September 14, 2026 · Sep 14, 2026–Sep 20, 2026 · published Sep 18, 2026

Two unrelated repos earn the featured treatment this week, both for tagged releases: prime-radiant-inc/teststrip cuts v0.3.0 and v0.3.1 behind a heavy scenario-testing push, and prime-radiant-inc/agentic-usage-meter ships v0.2.6 with a MiniMax quota fix. Underneath them, prime-radiant-inc/evener has its busiest week of either recent window, and prime-radiant-inc/clearance has an unusually open one — four separate outside contributors, each landing one PR, none of them repeats from any prior week’s post. Three more outside contributions turn up elsewhere too: prime-radiant-inc/awesome-superpowers (Matías Beccaria), prime-radiant-inc/prime-radiant-marketplace (caioniehues), and obra/superpowers (GoldJohnKing). This week is still open: the data below runs through Friday evening, not the full calendar week.
