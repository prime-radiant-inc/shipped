bot-toolkit and parallel-adversarial-review launch

Week of May 4, 2026 · May 4, 2026–May 10, 2026 · published May 10, 2026

A week of standing things up and shipping them immediately. bot-toolkit — a reusable TypeScript core for building unattended Claude-powered chat agents — was created and published to npm in the same seven days, and parallel-adversarial-review appeared as a pair of adversarial code-review skills, complete with a self-review pass using its own tooling.
