Superpowers 6 ships, claude-session-driver hits 4.0

Week of June 15, 2026 · Jun 15, 2026–Jun 21, 2026 · published Jun 21, 2026

A week of releases landing. Superpowers 6 shipped its full release train, claude-session-driver cut v4.0.0 — the release that ships the bash-to-TypeScript rewrite from the week before — and obol and slackline both stepped their versions. This is the last of the weeks we’re reaching back to fill in; the week of June 22 picks up right after it as our first originally-published recap.

The week in numbers

  • 15 repos active (5 featured, 10 also shipped)
  • 813 commits
  • 15 merged PRs
  • +269,320 / −79,189 lines
  • 4 contributors
  • 10 tagged releases

A few of this week’s tagged releases are incremental point releases within active release trains (Superpowers 6’s .1/.2/.3 follow-ups, obol’s and slackline’s steady cadence), so the release count reflects a busy publishing week rather than that many distinct milestones.

Featured

obra/superpowers

An agentic skills framework and software-development methodology that actually works.

17 commits 13 PRs 🏷 3 releases +838 −134

A full Superpowers 6 release train in one week: v6.0.0 unified the evals submodule across per-agent bootstrap scenarios and brought Prime Radiant branding to the visual companion; v6.0.1 fixed Codex plugin-version display; v6.0.2 stopped shipping the evals submodule after it turned out to break plugin installs; and v6.0.3 moved spec-driven-development artifacts out of the git-protected .git/ path into the working tree, with per-worktree isolation tests. (A “Job posting” commit also landed mid-week — recruiting, not code.)

obra/claude-session-driver

Launch, control, and monitor other Claude Code sessions as workers via tmux.

21 commits 1 PR +22,029 −3,489

v4.0.0 — the release that ships the previous week’s full TypeScript core rewrite (Claude parity plus Codex and Pi drivers). It came with follow-on fixes: Codex apply_patch rendering in read-turn, Pi tool-name casing normalized to the Bash/Read convention, an unregistered-derive-workers listing, a --last N cap on read-events --follow backlog, and a mixed-fleet HARNESS column in list.

prime-radiant-inc/obol

Reads an AI-agent transcript and estimates what it cost — a Rust core with bindings for Python, Go, and TypeScript.

4 commits 🏷 2 releases +924 −1,064

Releases v0.5.0 and v0.6.0. obol added an ATIF trajectory dialect and then — a breaking change — removed the per-agent raw-log dialects entirely, leaving ATIF and obol’s own dialect as the only two supported formats. Plus a cost-accuracy fix: an empty model now signals “unknown” rather than “unpriced,” and an explicit final_metrics.total_cost_usd overrides the rate-table lookup when present.

prime-radiant-inc/slackline

A single-binary Go CLI that gives AI agents a Slack identity.

12 commits 🏷 2 releases +2,457 −283

It also defaulted read/listen output to compact text and fixed a dropped message_replied parent-event bug plus a Socket Mode listener-failure visibility gap.
